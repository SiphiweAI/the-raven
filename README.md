How the Forecast Works

We look at your recent sales history.
For each product, we gather the last few sales quantities over time. For example, if you sold 10, 12, and 14 units over the last three days, that becomes our reference point.

We use a “moving average” to predict the next values.
The moving average is like taking the average of the last few days to guess the next day.

Example: If your last 3 days of sales were 10, 12, and 14 units:

Average = (10 + 12 + 14) ÷ 3 = 12 units

The system predicts 12 units for the next day.

Then it “slides” the window forward by one day and repeats the process to forecast multiple days ahead.

The forecast adapts to your trends.

If sales are slowly increasing, the moving average will reflect that upward trend.

If sales drop, the forecast also adjusts downward.

Forecasts are stored and visualized.

Each product gets its own forecast.

The chart shows your actual sales (solid line) and predicted sales (dashed line) so you can quickly see what to expect in the next days.
